About Mickey

Mickey Mouse is a cartoon character created by Walt Disney and animator Ub Iwerks. He is a cheerful, brave, and curious mouse who always tries to do the right thing. Mickey became the mascot of The Walt Disney Company and one of the most recognizable characters in the world.

Where Mickey Lives

In the cartoons, Mickey lives in Mouseton (also known as Mouse Town), a cheerful town full of talking animals. In the Mickey Mouse Clubhouse series, his gang hangs out at the Clubhouse, a big house shaped like his head.

You can also visit his house in real life! Mickey's House in Mickey's Toontown at Disneyland (California) lets guests walk through his home and meet him in person. Mickey also has homes at other Disney parks around the world, including Walt Disney World (Florida), Tokyo Disney Resort, Disneyland Paris, Hong Kong Disneyland, and Shanghai Disney Resort.

Mickey Through the Years

  • 1928 Mickey debuts. Plane Crazy is tested first; Steamboat Willie premieres on Nov 18 with synchronized sound.
  • 1929 Mickey starts talking, and Walt Disney provides his voice.
  • 1930 Mickey Mouse comic strip begins.
  • 1935 The Band Concert, Mickey's first color cartoon.
  • 1940 Fantasia: "The Sorcerer's Apprentice."
  • 1955 The Mickey Mouse Club airs; Disneyland opens in California.
  • 1978 Mickey gets a Hollywood Walk of Fame star (he's the first cartoon character to).
  • 1983 Mickey's Christmas Carol brings him back to the big screen.
  • 2006 Mickey Mouse Clubhouse launches for preschoolers.
  • 2013 A new series of short cartoons revives his classic slapstick style.
  • 2021 Mickey Mouse Funhouse arrives.
  • 2024 The original 1928 "Steamboat Willie" version enters the US public domain.
